# Editorial magazine

**For:** essays, storytelling, brand manifestos, campaigns, interviews, book/course launches.
**Feeling:** a printed magazine spread cut into posts. Typography is the image.

## Palette
- Newsprint #f2efe8 background, black #111 text, one editorial red #c8102e for rules and numbers.
- Photography in black & white or muted color with `multiply` blend over the paper.

## Type
- `playfair` 600–800 headlines, very large (12–22%), tight leading (0.95–1.05); italic for one emphasized word.
- `lora` body 400, 32–36px, line-height 1.5, left-aligned, ≤ 45 characters per line.
- Small caps labels and folios (`inter` 600, 0.16em tracking): "ISSUE 01", "PART II".

## Composition
- Signature move: one enormous headline crossing post 1→2 (letters cut by the seam is intentional here), with a red hairline rule running through all posts.
- Columns: body text set in a narrow column on one side, white space on the other.
- Pull quotes as `quote` elements with `surface` fill or transparent fill + red accent mark.
- Drop cap / huge numeral in red at opacity 1 on chapter posts.

## Avoid
Gradients, rounded "app" cards, emoji, center-aligned body text, more than one red element per post besides the rule.

## Recommended setup
`style: editorial-magazine` · template `connected-headline` or `quote-thread` · 5–8 portrait posts.
