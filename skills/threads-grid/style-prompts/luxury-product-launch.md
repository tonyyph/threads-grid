# Luxury product launch

**For:** premium skincare, supplements, tea, yến sào (bird's nest), đông trùng hạ thảo, ginseng, fragrance, gift sets.
**Feeling:** quiet confidence. The product is the hero; everything else is a frame.

## Palette
- Deep base (forest #14261e, oxblood #3a1214, ink navy #0f1a2b) **or** warm ivory (#f3ecdf) for a light version.
- One metallic-feeling accent: champagne #c9a96e / #d8c08a. Use it for hairlines, numbers, small caps — never for big fills.
- Text: ivory on dark, deep ink on light. Muted tone for secondary copy (~60% contrast).
- Background: `radial` from a slightly lighter center to the base, grain 0.04–0.08. This one background spans every post.

## Type
- Headings: `cormorant` (500–600) or `playfair` (500). Large and few words. Tracking −0.01em.
- Labels: `be-vietnam`/`inter` 600, uppercase, tracking 0.14–0.2em, 22–28px.
- Body: `be-vietnam` 400, 32–36px, line-height 1.45.

## Composition
- Product image **≥ 60% of post height**, transparent PNG, soft `shadow` (y 3% of post, blur 6–8%, rgba(0,0,0,.35)).
- Signature move: product on seam 1|2 standing in an `arch` shape (fill `secondary`, opacity .9) with a radial `gradient` glow (`accent` → transparent, opacity .3–.45) behind it.
- Thin champagne `line` (2–3px) through all posts at the same height = thread of continuity.
- Generous margins: safe area 8–9%. Max one headline + one line of body per post.
- Post rhythm: Promise → Origin/ingredient → Reveal (big product) → Benefit → Ritual (how to use) → Gift/CTA.

## Avoid
Gold gradients on text, more than one metallic, drop shadows on text, stock "luxury" ornaments, crowded benefit lists, discount stickers on hero posts.

## Recommended setup
`style: luxury-product-launch` · template `product-hero-split` · 4–6 square or portrait posts.
