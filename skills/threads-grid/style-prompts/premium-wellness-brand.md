# Premium wellness brand

**For:** health, nutrition, beauty, yoga, spa, clean supplements, herbal products.
**Feeling:** warm, calm, polished — a soft editorial spread.

## Palette
- Cream #f4ede3 → sand #ece2d3 linear background (angle ~100°), grain 0.05.
- Sage #7a8b6f and clay #b8866b as the only colors; text warm brown #3a332c.
- Surfaces: #fbf8f3 discs/cards with very soft shadows.

## Type
- Headings: `lora` 500 (italic for one emphasized word) or `cormorant` 500.
- Body/labels: `be-vietnam` 400/500. Labels small caps, tracking 0.12em.
- Line-height generous: headings 1.15, body 1.5.

## Composition
- Organic shapes: `ellipse`, `arch`, `pill` masks on photos. Soft `ring` outlines (1.5–2px clay).
- Signature move (3×3 puzzle): one large surface disc centered on the grid, product in the center post, an orbit `ring` and a `wave` line crossing the bottom row (template `puzzle-grid`).
- Carousel version: arch-masked lifestyle photo crossing seam 1|2, one benefit per post with a small sage `badge`.
- Lots of air: safe area 9–10%, max 25 words per post.

## Avoid
Neon, pure black, harsh contrast, clinical stock photos, more than two shape motifs, medical claims in headlines.

## Recommended setup
`style: premium-wellness-brand` · template `puzzle-grid` (3×3) or `product-hero-split` · square or portrait.
