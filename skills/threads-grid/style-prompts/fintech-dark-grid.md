# Fintech dark grid

**For:** fintech, banking, crypto, trading, analytics, dashboard and B2B data products.
**Feeling:** precise, trustworthy, premium-dark UI. Data first, glow second.

## Palette
- Background: radial #121a2e center → #05070d edges, grain 0.03.
- Surfaces #111827, borders rgba(255,255,255,.08), text #e8edf7, muted #7c879c.
- Primary blue #5b8cff; accent mint #3cf0c5 used **sparingly** (one number, one line, one CTA).

## Type
- `space-grotesk` 600–700 headings, tight tracking; `inter` body; `jetbrains` for numbers, tickers and labels.
- Big metrics: 14–22% of post width, tabular feel, accent color.

## Composition
- Product screenshots in `phone` or `browser` frames, cropping across seams, slight rotation (−4° to 4°).
- Signature move: one thin accent `line` (or `arc`) running through every post like a chart trendline; metric `badge`s sit on it (template `timeline`).
- Grid discipline: align everything to the 8% safe area; use `rect` surfaces with radius 24–32 and 1px stroke as cards.
- Glows: radial `gradient` primary → transparent at opacity ≤ 0.35, behind screenshots only.

## Avoid
Rainbow neon, fake 3D coins, stock "hacker" imagery, unreadable small numbers, more than one glow color per post.

## Recommended setup
`style: fintech-dark-grid` · template `timeline` or `connected-headline` · 5–7 square/portrait posts.
